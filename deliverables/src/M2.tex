\section{Current Approach and Changes Since M1}
\textbf{Unchanged:} the three-stage life simulation, money-battle screen, teach-after-the-choice loop with abstract animations, mistake bank, persona framing, difficulty buckets, before/after quiz, emailed report, NGO join codes, guardrail.

\textbf{Decided since M1} (M1 left these open):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Economy.} Everyone starts with $S=\rs5{,}000$ in-game per month in Stage~1; in-game price $=$ real price $\times S / $ monthly pocket money (band midpoints \rs200, \rs800, \rs2{,}000, \rs4{,}000 from the indirect weekly-spend question). Stages 2--3 use fixed reference incomes (\rs20{,}000 and \rs50{,}000 real $\to$ \rs25{,}000 and \rs60{,}000 in-game).
  \item \textbf{Time.} Ten monthly chapters: 4 (Intermediate) $+$ 3 (first salary) $+$ 3 (earning more), then the epilogue at 60. Stage jumps skip 5, 6 and 25 years so compounding is visible.
  \item \textbf{Happiness.} 0--100, starts at 70, decays 3 per chapter; choices move it $-30$ to $+15$; zero restarts the task; below 30 locks mini-games.
  \item \textbf{Mini-games.} Two, skippable: \emph{Kirana Rush} (sort needs from wants) and \emph{Scam or Legit} (swipe fake messages). A win pays 8\% of monthly income as a ``side gig'' and $+10$ happiness.
  \item \textbf{Hints and guide.} A guide character delivers lesson lines; hints switch on after two failures of the same task.
  \item \textbf{Second language.} Telugu for onboarding, HUD and two tasks; a Hindi file proves ``add a language $=$ add a file''.
  \item \textbf{Stack.} TypeScript + Phaser~3 + Vite (PWA, offline), Vitest, zod schema validation, a small Node report service (nodemailer $\to$ sandbox inbox), static NGO dashboard mock.
\end{itemize}
