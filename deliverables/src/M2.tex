\section{Current Approach and Changes Since M1}
\textbf{Unchanged:} three-stage life simulation, Pok\'emon-style money battles, teach-after-the-choice with abstract animations, mistake bank, persona framing, difficulty buckets, before/after quiz, emailed report, NGO join codes, the guardrail.

\textbf{Decided since M1} (M1 left these open):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Economy.} Everyone starts Stage~1 with $S=\rs5{,}000$ in-game per month; in-game price $=$ real price $\times S/$ monthly pocket money, with pocket money taken from the indirect weekly-spend band (\rs200, \rs800, \rs2{,}000, \rs4{,}000). Stages 2--3 use fixed reference incomes (\rs20{,}000 and \rs50{,}000 real $\rightarrow$ \rs25{,}000 and \rs60{,}000 in-game).
  \item \textbf{Time.} Ten monthly chapters: 4 (Intermediate) $+$ 3 (first salary) $+$ 3 (earning more), then the epilogue at 60. Stage jumps skip 5, 6 and 25 years, so compounding on the four made-up assets is visible.
  \item \textbf{Happiness.} 0--100, starts at 70, decays 3 per chapter; choices move it $-30\ldots+15$; zero restarts the task; below 30 locks mini-games.
  \item \textbf{Mini-games.} Two, skippable: \emph{Kirana Rush} (sort needs from wants) and \emph{Scam or Legit} (swipe fake messages). A win pays 8\% of monthly income as a side gig and $+10$ happiness.
  \item \textbf{Guide and hints.} A guide character delivers the lesson line; hints switch on after two failures of the same task.
  \item \textbf{Languages.} Telugu for the whole UI (158/159 keys) and for onboarding, quiz, persona cards and two demo tasks; a Hindi file with only the title and buttons proves ``add a language $=$ add a file'' with English fallback.
  \item \textbf{Stack.} TypeScript + Phaser~3 + Vite (PWA), Vitest, zod schema validation; a small Node report service; a static NGO dashboard mock. One generic battle screen is driven entirely by JSON content.
\end{itemize}

\section{Experiments Run So Far and Results}
\textbf{Economy scaler check} (unit-tested): a \rs200 cricket bat costs \rs200 / \rs400 / \rs1{,}000 in-game for students with \rs5{,}000 / \rs2{,}500 / \rs1{,}000 of monthly pocket money, i.e.\ the same share of their money as in real life.

\textbf{Scripted players on the real content} (20 seeds per cell, same seeds across cells). Three policies play all ten chapters: \emph{always good}, \emph{random}, \emph{always worst} (which never repeats a fatal choice on a restart, so it can finish).

Reading: good play ends stable and literate; careless play detours 1--2 times and sometimes goes broke; the worst play restarts several times, can go broke (an uninsured \rs2 lakh hospital bill), yet always finishes. The first run of this table showed \emph{good} play scoring literate only 20--80\% and nobody ever going broke; both were fixed (NCFE evidence rule; a genuinely expensive trap) and re-measured.

\textbf{Unit tests: 124 passing} --- engine 68 (RNG reproducibility, scaler, delayed/repeating effects, task selection with pillar rotation and the investing gate, restart/detour routing, mistake bank, investing returns, NCFE scoring, what-if determinism, save/resume round-trip), content 24, mini-games 20, game helpers 11, report 1.

\textbf{Content validation:} 24 main tasks (12 / 6 / 6 per stage), 72 persona framings, 9 detours, 16 random events, 17 explainer animations, 17 concepts, 38 delayed or repeating effects; every NCFE item (17/17) has at least one tagged choice; every cross-reference (concept, explainer, detour, place, event, flag) resolves; a double-blind string check passes.

\textbf{Devices and network} (production build, headless Chromium, 360$\times$640): Fast 3G + 4$\times$ CPU throttle: \textbf{5.1 s} to first frame on first visit, \textbf{1.4 s} on a repeat visit; Slow 3G: 20.5 s (538 KB transferred). \textbf{Offline reload: 0.5 s} via the service worker. The M1 target of 5 s held only on Fast 3G; on Slow 3G the bundle must shrink (asset trimming cut the precache from 1,638 to 65 files; Phaser itself is the floor).

\textbf{Report pipeline:} a seeded game produced a PDF report emailed to a sandbox inbox with the guardian in CC; with no network the service stores the message locally and the app offers a download instead.

\textbf{Play test:} unguided peer play of Stage~1 on a phone is scheduled for the 02:45 integration session; results (time to first battle, help requests, drop-off) go into the final report.
